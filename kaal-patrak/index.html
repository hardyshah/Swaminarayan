કાળ Patrak

A calendar for 1,000,000 years. First layer: Shri Swaminarayan's life, day by day.

Sources. Daily charitra for April to October 1781 is transcribed from શ્રી સ્વામિનારાયણમ્ (Shri Swaminarayanam), ed. Shastri Swami Nirgundasji, Shri Sahajanand Gurukul, Asarva, 2015, pages 1–3 and 6–7; the text is the book's and remains its copyright. Vachanamrut days and life events come from the Swaminarayan Dinacharya collection (anirdesh.com Vachanamrut, swaminarayanbhagwan.org, swaminarayan.org, swaminarayan.nu, SKSST timeline). Where no source gives the tithi, it is computed from modern sun and moon positions at sunrise (Chhapaiya until Aug 1799, Gadhada after) and marked ગણતરી; traditional panchangs can differ by one tithi near a kshaya or repeated tithi. Weekdays and English dates use the Gregorian calendar, extended backwards before 1582. Events added with “Record an event” are shared with everyone who can open this page.
