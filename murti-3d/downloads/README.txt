Shri Swaminarayan murti 3D print files (free, for personal devotional use)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is a stylised, hand-sculpted 3D model made from public darshan photographs.
It is NOT a scan or photogrammetric copy of the murti, so proportions and ornaments are approximate.

File: binary STL, units = millimetres, already scaled to the height in the file name
(height includes the pedestal and the golden arch/prabha behind the murti).
The mesh is one closed, watertight 2-manifold shell (checked with trimesh and manifold3d).

Printing tips
- Print upright on the pedestal. Supports are needed under the flute/raised hand, the crown crest and the garland.
- 4"-9": resin (0.03-0.05 mm layers) gives the best face detail; FDM works with a 0.2-0.4 mm nozzle.
- 12"-24": FDM at 0.12-0.2 mm layers, 10-15% infill, 3-4 walls.
- 36" and 1 m: larger than most printers; cut the model into parts in your slicer
  (e.g. PrusaSlicer / Bambu Studio / Cura "cut" tool), print hollow with 4+ walls and low infill, then glue and fill seams.
  For resin, hollow the model and add drain holes in the base.
- Paint: black-stone gloss for Gopinathji Maharaj, warm skin tone for Ghanshyam Maharaj, gold for the arch and ornaments.
